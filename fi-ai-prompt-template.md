# Fi AI Assistant - Personalized Financial Planning Prompt

## 🧠 Ready-to-Use Prompt Template

Copy and paste this template into Fi's AI assistant ("Talk to AI" / "Ask Fi"), filling in your personal details:

```
Hi! I'm [your age]-year-old [profession—e.g., software engineer / freelance designer / business owner] earning around ₹[monthly income]. I currently have:

* ₹[balance] in my Fi Savings account
* A SIP of ₹[amount] in mutual funds
* ₹[amount] invested in US stocks

My financial goals are:

1. Build an emergency fund covering 6 months of expenses (~₹[target emergency corpus])
2. Save ₹[target amount] for a vacation in [month, year]
3. Grow my investments to reach ₹[target corpus] by [year]
4. Understand if I'm eligible for Fi's ₹5 L instant loan and what repayment options suit me

Could you please:

* Propose a short-term and long-term savings/investment roadmap
* Recommend automated tools like FIT Rules, AutoSave, and AutoInvest to help me stay on track
* Optimise my asset mix between mutual funds vs US stocks
* Explain how to fully leverage Fi's zero-forex debit card and rewards program
```

## 💡 Personalization Tips

### Basic Customization
- Replace all `[placeholders]` with your actual figures (income, balances, goals, dates)

- **Risk Preference**: Specify your comfort level—e.g., "I'm comfortable with moderate risk" or "I prefer conservative investments"
- **Current Challenges**: Mention specific pain points like "struggling to save consistently" or "confused about tax implications"

- Education funding (self or children)
- Retirement corpus building
- Tax optimization strategies
- Insurance coverage planning
